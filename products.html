ATELIER HANDBOOK & COMMITMENT

The Art of Cooking Fresh Pasta

Fresh handmade egg pasta cooks in under 3 minutes. Follow our atelier chef's guide for a perfect al dente table.

🌊
STEP 01

SALTY MEDITERRANEAN WATER

Bring 4 quarts of water to a rolling boil per pound of fresh pasta. Add 1 tbsp coarse sea salt so the water tastes like ocean water.

⏱️
STEP 02

2-MINUTE FLASH COOK

Drop ribbons or filled ravioli gently into boiling water. Fresh pasta floats to the top when tender and al dente in just 120–180 seconds.

🧈
STEP 03

STARCHY EMULSION

Reserve 1/2 cup of hot starchy cooking water before draining. Swirl water with warm sauce to bind the sauce seamlessly to the pasta ribbons.

🧀
STEP 04

FINISH OFF THE HEAT

Toss pasta with sauce off the flame. Finish with a drizzle of cold-pressed EVOO, freshly grated Parmigiano 24M, and torn basil leaves.
