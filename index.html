THE ART OF FRESH PASTA

Every sheet begins with dough, time and a practiced hand.

We prepare our dough every morning using 100% durum wheat semolina flour and fresh pasture eggs. The dough rests to allow the gluten to soften before being hand-rolled and cut through classic brass dies.
