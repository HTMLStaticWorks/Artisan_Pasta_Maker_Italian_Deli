SAMPLE DAILY COUNTER

Featured Counter Highlights

Sample demonstration selection. All items subject to seasonal availability at the deli.

HOUSE SAUCE

HOUSE TOMATO SAUCE

San Marzano passata simmered 4 hours with fresh basil.

Suggested use: Tagliatelle or Gnocchi base
SAUCE SPECIFICATION

HOUSE TOMATO SAUCE

Origin: San Marzano DOP Tomatoes, Campania
Craft: Slowly simmered in copper pots for 4 hours with cold-pressed EVOO & crushed garlic.
Chef's Note: 100% natural tomato sweetness with balanced acidity. Never sweetened with sugar.
HOUSE PESTO

BASIL PESTO

Pounded basil leaves, pine nuts, garlic, and Ligurian olive oil.

Suggested use: Orecchiette coat or crostini spread
PESTO SPECIFICATION

BASIL PESTO

Origin: Genovese Basil DOP, Liguria
Craft: Pounded basil blended with toasted pine nuts, Parmigiano, & Pecorino Sardo.
Chef's Note: Emulsify with starchy warm pasta water when coating hot orecchiette.
IMPORTED CHEESE

PARMESAN (24 MONTHS)

Nutty, crystalline aged Parmigiano-Reggiano cut from whole wheels.

Suggested use: Freshly grated atop warm pasta
CHEESE SPECIFICATION

PARMESAN (24 MONTHS)

Origin: Reggio Emilia, Italy
Craft: Aged 24 full months with crunchy tyrosine crystals & rich butterscotch aroma.
Chef's Note: Shave paper-thin over arugula salad or grate fine over handmade tagliatelle.
ANTIPASTI

MARINATED VEGETABLES

Artichoke hearts, sun-dried tomatoes, and sweet baby peppers.

Suggested use: Dinner starter platter
ANTIPASTI SPECIFICATION

MARINATED VEGETABLES

Origin: Puglia & Roman Countryside
Craft: Roman artichokes, sun-dried tomatoes, & sweet peppers in herbs & white balsamic.
Chef's Note: Serve at room temperature alongside freshly toasted sourdough focaccia slices.
PANTRY SPECIALTY

CASTELVETRANO OLIVES

Bright green Sicilian olives cured in light brine with citrus zest & bay leaf.

Suggested use: Aperitivo snack or pasta salad finish
OLIVE SPECIFICATION

CASTELVETRANO OLIVES

Origin: Castelvetrano, Sicily
Craft: Crisp, buttery, non-bitter cure tossed with orange zest & wild fennel seeds.
Chef's Note: Pair with chilled Vermentino or prosecco for an authentic Italian aperitivo.
ARTISAN CONDIMENT

TRUFFLE BALSAMICO

Aged Modena balsamic vinegar infused with black winter truffle essence.

Suggested use: Drizzled over Parmigiano, meats or gnocchi
CONDIMENT SPECIFICATION

TRUFFLE BALSAMICO

Origin: Modena, Emilia-Romagna
Craft: Aged 12 years in oak barrels, infused with wild black winter truffles.
Chef's Note: A single light drizzle transforms potato gnocchi or Parmigiano into gourmet luxury.
ATELIER COUNTER SERVICES

How Our Deli Service Works

Beyond raw provisions: experience our 3-step personalized service for home cooks, hostesses, and table gatherings.

01
BESPOKE CUTS

Precision Weight & Slicing

Whole wheels of Parmigiano, Pecorino, and cured salumi sliced paper-thin or portioned to your exact gram specification right before your eyes.

  • ✓ Cut fresh to order by our cheesemongers
  • ✓ Zero pre-shredded or oxidized fillers
  • ✓ Custom charcuterie board planning
02
SEALED FRESHNESS

Cold Pack & Glass Jars

House passata, basil pesto, and marinated artichokes sealed in airtight glass jars and thermal packs to preserve peak aroma for 7+ days at home.

  • ✓ Recyclable Italian glass jars

  • ✓ 7-to-10 day domestic freshness guarantee

CHEF GUIDANCE
