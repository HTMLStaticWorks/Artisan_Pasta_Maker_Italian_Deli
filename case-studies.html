Crafting an Immersive 4-Course Tableside Truffle Experience

When our hosts planned their milestone autumn celebration, they wanted to move far beyond standard banquet catering. Their goal was an interactive atelier where guests could smell fresh semolina dough, watch pasta cut live, and savor hot courses finished tableside.

🌾

Live Bronze-Die Extrusion Station

Guests gathered around our mobile wooden pasta bench as 40-yolk pasture egg dough was extruded into warm golden ribbons.

🍄

Direct D.O.P. Truffle Sourcing

Flown directly from Piedmont within 48 hours and shaved tableside over mountain sage butter tagliatelle.

🍷

Sommelier-Paired Cellar Reserves

Each pasta course was matched with cellar-aged Barolo and crisp Franciacorta chosen to elevate the dough's richness.
