OUR ATELIER HERITAGE

Handmade with Heart. Shaped by Italian Care.

From early morning flour dusting across wooden tables to slow bronze-die extrusions, Pasta Forma preserves the centuries-old art of authentic Italian pasta making.

🌾 100% Italian Durum Semolina
THE ORIGIN & PASSION

From Bologna to New York:

Pasta Forma was founded on a simple conviction: that true pasta is not a mass-produced commodity, but a living craft. Inspired by the legendary sfoglini of Emilia-Romagna, we set out to build an authentic pasta atelier where golden egg ribbons and bronze-die extruded shapes are created fresh every single morning.

✨ Hand-Rolled Sfoglia
🥉 Bronze-Die Cut

CRAFT & TECHNIQUE

How We Make Our Pasta

A step-by-step look into the morning ritual of our artisan pasta kitchen.

01
STEP 01 — SELECTION

Pure Italian Ingredients

Coarse durum semolina from Italian fields paired with 40 pasture-raised egg yolks per kg for rich, golden elasticity.

🌾 Non-GMO Durum Semolina
02
STEP 02 — ROLLING

Wooden Board Kneading

Dough is hand-kneaded, rested for 45 minutes, then rolled across untreated wood to create a porous, sauce-gripping surface.

🪵 Untreated Wood Texture
03
STEP 03 — EXTRUSION

Bronze Die Shaping

Extruded slowly through custom bronze dies at cool temperatures to preserve nutrient integrity and rough exterior texture.

🥉 Custom Bronze Dies
04
STEP 04 — CUT & PACK

7:00 AM Fresh Batch

Hand-cut ribbons are inspected by our chef, packed into breathable eco-boxes, and ready for same-day delivery or counter pickup.

⏱️ 7:00 AM Daily Fresh Cut
OUR ARTISAN CREW

Meet the Pasta Makers & Culinary Team

The passionate chefs, sfoglini masters, and deli curators behind Pasta Forma.

FOUNDER & MASTER SFOGLINO

Chef Matteo Rossi

Trained under legendary sfoglini in Bologna for 15 years. Matteo oversees dough formulation, wooden board rolling, and exact yolk-to-flour ratios every morning.

HEAD DELI & CHARCUTERIE CURATOR

Lucia Romano

Directly imports 24-month Parmigiano-Reggiano, DOP Prosciutto di Parma, and artisanal olives from small Italian producers for our neighborhood deli counter.

SAUCES & CULINARY CHEF

Giancarlo Bianchi

Slow-simmers our signature 4-hour San Marzano tomato passatas, wild boar ragùs, and pounded Genovese basil pestos in small batch copper pots.
